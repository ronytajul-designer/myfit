Still have a question? Ask us
Is MyFit free to use?

Yes. The Free plan covers 25 quizzes a month with no credit card. Unlimited is $4/month for unlimited quizzes, with a 14-day free trial. You can switch or uninstall anytime.

Will MyFit slow down my store?

No. MyFit adds about DATA: KB, only on product pages that use the block, and loads after your page content. Shoppers see your page at the same speed.

Does it work with my theme?

MyFit works with any Online Store 2.0 theme, including Dawn and all free Shopify themes. VERIFY: vintage themes

Do I need a developer?

No. Install from the Shopify App Store, drag the MyFit block into place in the theme editor, and you're live, usually in a couple of minutes. No code.

How is this different from a size chart?

A size chart hands shoppers a grid and asks them to measure themselves. MyFit asks a few short questions (gender, age, height, weight and fit preference) and gives one recommended size for that product.

Does MyFit work for all types of apparel?

Yes for clothing: tops, bottoms, dresses, outerwear, swimwear and more. Each garment maps to a body zone, and you choose the measurements that matter. VERIFY: footwear, kids
